Task E1 Product proxy

Implement GET /products/{product_id} and downstream GET /products/{product_id}, with typed id/name/price response and 404 propagation. Require returned id to match the requested product, a string name and a finite nonnegative numeric price. Give the same schema and clean examples to C0 and C1. Malformed/mismatched success responses must not become accepted products. 

The general downstream product schema is {"id": integer, "name": string, "price": finite nonnegative number}. Example: GET /products/7 returns {"id":7,"name":"Keyboard","price":49.5}; other product identifiers have their own corresponding records.
