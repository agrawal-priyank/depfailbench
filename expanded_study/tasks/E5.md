Task E5 Order/payment

Implement POST /orders/{order_id}/pay, downstream POST /charges with order_id, provider Idempotency-Key semantics, confirmed charge receipt, public order_id/status=PAID/charge_id, and app.state.orders. Retain PENDING, PAID, FAILED/UNPAID and UNRESOLVED meanings from the corrected original contract. A committed charge with lost acknowledgment must not cause another charge, false PAID status, or a claim of definite failure without a charge. Validate against provider charge records as well as application state; never infer safety solely from an HTTP error.

The provider supports the Idempotency-Key header: repeated charge requests with one key and the same order return the original receipt without another charge. Its receipt is {"charge_id": string, "status":"captured"}. The public success body is {"order_id": string, "status":"PAID", "charge_id": string}. Expose app.state.orders keyed by order_id; PAID requires confirmed receipt, FAILED/UNPAID require confirmed failure without a charge, UNRESOLVED means outcome unknown, and PENDING is permitted initially.
