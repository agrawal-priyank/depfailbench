# Versioned research dataset metadata

The expanded research dataset **v2.0.0** is published and publicly accessible at
[10.5281/zenodo.23626768](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23626768). The companion
software **v1.2.0** is published at
[10.5281/zenodo.23627089](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23627089) and in the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/agrawal-priyank/depfailbench/releases/tag/v1.2.0).
Both records were published on 24 October 2026.

The [v2.0.0 directory](v2.0.0/README.md) preserves the exact metadata, inventory,
license and checksum files prepared for that version. Its `metadata.json` and
`UPLOAD_MANIFEST.json` retain their original prepublication status wording as
provenance; that wording does not describe current public availability. This
main-branch guide records the completed publication without rewriting those
archival preparation files or the deposited assets.

The dataset payloads, including `Supplementary_Material_S2.zip`, are distributed
through the version-specific dataset record rather than the source repository.
The versioned inventory/checksum files describe those deposited payloads; they
are not a promise that every payload is checked into Git. Use the deposit's
original files and hashes when reproducing the reported analysis.

For the default saved-record computation, see
[reproduction instructions](../docs/REPRODUCING_ARRAY_REVISION.md). It reads
recorded evidence without importing or executing generated services. Historical
320-program data, superseded sources, validation records and the expanded cohort
remain separately identified. No unsuccessful outcome or repeat flag is removed.

New corrections or data additions require a new version. Older tags, files and
DOI versions remain unchanged under the project's
[version-preservation policy](../docs/VERSION_PRESERVATION.md).
